# Glossary (yougoshuu)

Last Verified: 2026-09-08

> This file is the shared terminology reference for all skills in the `ekyc-jp` plugin.
> Once all 19 skills were complete, it was supplemented with the terms that actually appear in each skill.
> For the **names, designations, and timing** of identity verification methods (ho / he / ka / ru, etc., and effective dates),
> `references/method-map.md` and `skills/honnin-kakunin-houhou/SKILL.md` are the sole authoritative sources,
> and this file does not make definitive statements about them (wording is aligned with method-map.md).
> For precise values such as PIN length, lockout attempt counts, and AIDs, refer to the individual skills
> (`pin-lock-handling` / `references/apdu-cheatsheet.md`, etc.).

| Public Personal Authentication Service (公的個人認証サービス) | こうてきこじんにんしょうサービス | A public service for identity authentication and tamper detection using the electronic certificates on the My Number Card's IC chip. In English, JPKI. | jpki-overview |
| Signing certificate (署名用電子証明書) | しょめいようでんししょうめいしょ | A certificate for applying electronic signatures to documents. Contains the four basic attributes. PIN of 6–16 characters. | jpki-ap-shomei, signature-verification |
| User-authentication certificate (利用者証明用電子証明書) | りようしゃしょうめいようでんししょうめいしょ | For authenticating the holder, e.g., at login. Does not contain the four basic attributes. 4-digit PIN. | jpki-ap-riyousha |

| Card-face verification application (券面事項確認AP) | けんめんじこうかくにんAP | An application for retrieving the card-face image, facial photo, and Individual Number. | kenmen-jikou-kakunin-ap |
| Revocation (失効) | しっこう | A certificate losing its validity (due to expiration, change of address, change of name, death, etc.). | jlis-yukousei-kakunin |
| Competent minister's certification (主務大臣認定) | しゅむだいじんにんてい | The certification that a business operator performing signature verification itself must obtain. | platform-jigyousha |
| Platform operator (プラットフォーム事業者) | プラットフォームじぎょうしゃ | A certified business operator that provides certificate validity verification functions to other business operators. PF operator. | platform-jigyousha |
| Service provider operator (サービスプロバイダ事業者) | サービスプロバイダじぎょうしゃ | A business operator that outsources signature verification to a PF operator and uses it. SP operator. | platform-jigyousha |
| J-LIS (J-LIS) | ジェイリス | Japan Agency for Local Authority Information Systems. The certificate issuer and trust anchor. | jpki-overview, jlis-yukousei-kakunin |

| OCSP (OCSP) | オーシーエスピー | A protocol for querying the validity of an individual certificate in real time. | jlis-yukousei-kakunin |
| Act on Prevention of Transfer of Criminal Proceeds (犯収法) | はんしゅうほう | The Act on Prevention of Transfer of Criminal Proceeds. | honnin-kakunin-houhou |
| Method "ka" (カ方式) | カほうしき | An identity verification method using the JPKI signing certificate (currently Article 6, Paragraph 1, Item 1 (ka) of the Ordinance for Enforcement of the Act on Prevention of Transfer of Criminal Proceeds; from April 2027, new (nu)). Limited to cases where the specified business operator is the signature verifier (proviso). *Method designations and timing are based on `method-map.md` / `hourei-eKYC.md` as the authoritative sources. | honnin-kakunin-houhou |
| Method "he" (ヘ方式) | ヘほうしき | IC reading of an identity document + capturing the person's appearance. *Method designations and timing are based on `method-map.md` as the authoritative source. | honnin-kakunin-houhou, kenmen-nyuryoku-hojo-ap |
| Method "ru" (ル方式) | ルほうしき | A method using card-substitute electromagnetic records (Apple Wallet, etc.). *Method designations and timing are based on `method-map.md` as the authoritative source. | honnin-kakunin-houhou |
| Non-forwardable mail (転送不要郵便) | てんそうふようゆうびん | Mail that is not permitted to be forwarded. Used to confirm that an address actually exists. | honnin-kakunin-houhou |
| APDU (APDU) | エーピーディーユー | The unit of communication with an IC card (ISO 7816). | ios-corenfc-apdu |
| AID (AID) | エーアイディー | Application identifier. Specified with SELECT. | ios-corenfc-apdu, apdu-cheatsheet |
| SW1SW2 (SW1SW2) | エスダブリューいちにー | The status word of an APDU response. `90 00` = success. | pin-lock-handling |
| Consent information (同意情報) | どういじょうほう | The record of the individual's consent to the provision of the latest four attributes. Valid for 10 years. | doui-jouhou-kanri |
| Expiration date (有効期限) | ゆうこうきげん | The expiration date of the My Number Card itself and of its electronic certificates. One of the unlocking factors for the card-face input-assist application. The card itself and the electronic certificates have different expiration dates, and an electronic certificate is revoked when it reaches its expiration date even if the four basic attributes have not changed. For the specific number of years and the starting point, see kenmen-nyuryoku-hojo-ap / jlis-yukousei-kakunin. | kenmen-nyuryoku-hojo-ap, jlis-yukousei-kakunin |
| Security code (セキュリティコード) | セキュリティコード | A numeric code printed on the card face, used to unlock the card-face input-assist application. For details on the number of digits and where it is printed, see kenmen-nyuryoku-hojo-ap. | kenmen-nyuryoku-hojo-ap |
| Serial number (発行番号) | はっこうばんごう | An identification number assigned to each electronic certificate. Used by J-LIS for revocation checks via CRL and OCSP. | jlis-yukousei-kakunin, signature-verification |
| Article 3 of the Electronic Signatures Act (電子署名法第3条) | でんししょめいほうだいさんじょう | Article 3 of the Act on Electronic Signatures and Certification Business (presumption of authentic creation of electromagnetic records). An electromagnetic record bearing an electronic signature by the individual that meets certain requirements is presumed to have been authentically created. Electronic signatures made with a signing certificate can fall within its scope. | signature-verification, jpki-ap-shomei |
| Gaiji / non-standard characters (外字) | がいじ | Characters used in names and addresses that cannot be represented in standard character encodings. The card-face verification application and method "he" retrieve gaiji as images. | kenmen-jikou-kakunin-ap |
| Former surname (旧氏) | きゅううじ | The surname before marriage, etc. It can be shown alongside the current name on the My Number Card upon the individual's application, and it affects matching of the card face and name notation. | kihon-4-jouhou-doui, kenmen-jikou-kakunin-ap |
| Furigana (ふりがな) | ふりがな | The phonetic reading of a name. Following the amendment of the Family Register Act, it is now recorded in the family register and on the My Number Card. Pay attention to normalizing notation variants when matching. For details such as the effective date, see kihon-4-jouhou-doui. | kihon-4-jouhou-doui |
| Moving abroad (国外転出) | こくがいてんしゅつ | Relocating out of Japan. Following a system amendment, people who move abroad can now continue to use the My Number Card and electronic certificates. For details on revocation conditions and availability, see jlis-yukousei-kakunin. | jlis-yukousei-kakunin |
| Card-face verification PIN (券面事項確認用暗証番号) | けんめんじこうかくにんようあんしょうばんごう | The PIN used when reading card-face items (card-face image, facial photo, etc.) via the card-face verification application. For details on the number of digits and lockout attempt count, see pin-lock-handling / kenmen-jikou-kakunin-ap. | kenmen-jikou-kakunin-ap, pin-lock-handling |
| Individual Number PIN (個人番号用暗証番号) | こじんばんごうようあんしょうばんごう | The PIN used when reading the Individual Number (My Number) via the card-face verification application. For details on the number of digits and lockout attempt count, see kenmen-jikou-kakunin-ap / pin-lock-handling. | kenmen-jikou-kakunin-ap, pin-lock-handling |
| Digital Authentication App (デジタル認証アプリ) | デジタルにんしょうアプリ | An official app and API provided by the Digital Agency for authentication and retrieval of the four basic attributes using the My Number Card's electronic certificates. | ekyc-jp, jpki-overview |
| Myna App (マイナアプリ) | マイナアプリ | The common name for the official smartphone app for accessing Mynaportal, etc. Because names and functions are being reorganized, confirm the official name against primary sources as of each point in time. | ekyc-jp |
| App Clip (App Clip) | アップクリップ | A mechanism on iOS that lets users launch part of an app's functionality without installing the full app. Sometimes used as a lightweight entry point for eKYC flows (note constraints such as whether NFC reading is possible). | ios-nfc-ux-errors, ekyc-session-orchestration |
| NFCTagReaderSession (NFCTagReaderSession) | エヌエフシータグリーダーセッション | An iOS CoreNFC API class for APDU communication with ISO 7816 and other IC cards. Used for reading the My Number Card. | ios-corenfc-apdu, ios-nfc-ux-errors |
| PKCS#1 (PKCS#1) | ピーケーシーエスワンまたはピーケーシーエスいち | The standard for RSA cryptography key and signature formats (RFC 8017). Used in JPKI electronic signature verification. | signature-verification, ios-security |
| Individual Number (個人番号) | こじんばんごう | My Number. 12 digits. Not used in JPKI authentication, but can be retrieved via the card-face verification application. | kenmen-jikou-kakunin-ap |
| Juki AP / Basic Resident Register application (住基AP) | じゅうきエーピー | The Basic Resident Register application on the My Number Card. Used for identity verification related to the Basic Resident Register Network, etc. | mynumber-card-ic |
| Electronic signature (電子署名) | でんししょめい | A measure applied to an electromagnetic record that makes it possible to confirm the identity of its creator and that it has not been tampered with. Generated with the private key corresponding to the signing certificate. | jpki-ap-shomei, signature-verification |
| Notation variants (表記揺れ) | ひょうきゆれ | The phenomenon of the same name or address appearing in multiple notations (old vs. new character forms, differences in chome/banchi notation, etc.). Normalization is required when matching the four basic attributes. | kihon-4-jouhou-doui |
| Trust anchor (トラストアンカー) | トラストアンカー | The root of trust in a certificate chain. In JPKI, this is J-LIS, the issuer of the electronic certificates. | jpki-overview, signature-verification |
| Validity check (有効性確認) | ゆうこうせいかくにん | Confirming through J-LIS (CRL provision method / OCSP responder method) that an electronic certificate provided by a customer has not been revoked. | jlis-yukousei-kakunin, platform-jigyousha |
| Holder authentication (当人認証) | とうにんにんしょう | Confirming that the person performing authentication is the legitimate holder of the electronic certificate. Mainly handled by the user-authentication certificate. | jpki-ap-riyousha, honnin-kakunin-houhou |
| Identity proofing (身元確認) | みもとかくにん | Confirming that the person actually exists and that the applicant is that very person. Required, for example, for verification at the time of transaction under the Act on Prevention of Transfer of Criminal Proceeds. | honnin-kakunin-houhou |
| Selfie (セルフィー) | セルフィー | An image of the individual's appearance captured on the spot. In document-photo methods, it is matched against the facial photo on the identity document. | honnin-kakunin-houhou |
| Liveness (ライブネス) | ライブネス | Detection that the subject being captured is a real living person rather than a photo, video, mask, etc. A countermeasure against impersonation. | honnin-kakunin-houhou, ekyc-session-orchestration |
| Injection attack (インジェクション攻撃) | インジェクションこうげき | An impersonation attack that injects fake video or data into the camera input or communication path. Countermeasures are needed for both liveness and NFC reading. | ios-security, honnin-kakunin-houhou |
| Smartphone electronic certificate (スマホ用電子証明書) | スマホようでんししょうめいしょ | The functionality of the My Number Card's electronic certificates installed on a smartphone. Launched on May 11, 2023 (jpki-introduction.ja.md). | jpki-overview, ekyc-jp |
| My Number Card In-Person Verification App (マイナンバーカード対面確認アプリ) | マイナンバーカードたいめんかくにんアプリ | An app provided by the Digital Agency for verifying the authenticity of card-face items in person. | mynumber-card-ic |
| PIN (PIN) | ピン | Personal identification number (暗証番号). The My Number Card has multiple PINs for different purposes, and each becomes blocked (locked) after a certain number of consecutive incorrect entries. For attempt counts and how to unlock, see pin-lock-handling. | pin-lock-handling |
| Blocked / locked (閉塞) | へいそく | The state in which an AP has become unusable due to consecutive incorrect PIN entries. Unlocking and resetting require procedures at a municipal office counter, etc. | pin-lock-handling, ios-nfc-ux-errors |
| ISO/IEC 7816-4 (ISO/IEC 7816-4) | アイエスオーななはちいちろくのよん | The international standard that defines the structure of APDU commands and responses. | ios-corenfc-apdu |
| ISO/IEC 14443 (ISO/IEC 14443) | アイエスオーいちよんよんよんさん | The wireless communication standard for proximity IC cards (Type A / Type B). The My Number Card is Type B (for details, see apdu-cheatsheet.md / ios-corenfc-apdu). | ios-corenfc-apdu, ios-nfc-ux-errors |
| SELECT FILE (SELECT FILE) | セレクトファイル | The APDU command for selecting an AP (by AID) or an EF. | ios-corenfc-apdu |
| VERIFY (VERIFY) | ベリファイ | The APDU command for verifying a PIN. On an incorrect entry, the number of remaining attempts is reportedly returned in the status word (e.g., `63 CX`; JPKI-specific behavior needs verification; for details, see apdu-cheatsheet.md / ios-corenfc-apdu). | pin-lock-handling, ios-corenfc-apdu |
| COMPUTE DIGITAL SIGNATURE (COMPUTE DIGITAL SIGNATURE) | コンピュートデジタルシグネチャ | The APDU command that has the IC card generate a signature value using the private key stored on the card. | jpki-ap-shomei, ios-corenfc-apdu |
| Latest User Information (Four Attributes) Provision Service (最新の利用者情報（4情報）提供サービス) | さいしんのりようしゃじょうほうよんじょうほうていきょうサービス | A service in which J-LIS provides the latest four basic attributes online, based on the individual's consent. Launched on May 16, 2023 (jpki-introduction.ja.md). | doui-jouhou-kanri, kihon-4-jouhou-doui |
| Consent Acquisition Support Service (本人同意の取得支援サービス) | ほんにんどういのしゅとくしえんサービス | A Digital Agency service that uses Mynaportal to obtain the individual's consent required for the four-attribute provision service. Launched on October 29, 2024 (jpki-introduction.ja.md). | doui-jouhou-kanri |
| Primary sources (一次資料) | いちしりょう | Original sources such as the text of laws and regulations and materials issued by government agencies (e-Gov, National Police Agency JAFIC, Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications, Financial Services Agency, Digital Agency, etc.). Distinguish these from secondary information such as vendor materials, and confirm implementation and legal decisions against primary sources. | sources-refresh |
| Face ID / Touch ID (Face ID / Touch ID) | フェイスアイディー／タッチアイディー | iPhone biometric authentication. Used for holder authentication in the method using a My Number Card installed on a smartphone (method "ru"). | honnin-kakunin-houhou, ios-security |
| Inquiry number (照会番号) | しょうかいばんごう | A number entered when reading the Individual Number, etc., via the card-face verification application (printed on a copy of the resident record, etc.). | kenmen-jikou-kakunin-ap |
| Public-key cryptography (公開鍵暗号方式) | こうかいかぎあんごうほうしき | A scheme that uses two different keys (private key and public key) for encryption and decryption. The foundation of JPKI communication and signatures. The private key is held inside the My Number Card's IC chip. | signature-verification, ios-security |
| Certificate verification fee (電子証明書検証手数料) | でんししょうめいしょけんしょうてすうりょう | A usage-based fee that PF operators pay to J-LIS according to the number of validity checks (currently subject to a temporary waiver). | platform-jigyousha, jlis-yukousei-kakunin |
| Verification number A (照合番号A) | しょうごうばんごうエー | A verification value based on the 12-digit My Number printed on the card face, used when reading the Individual Number (My Number) side via the card-face verification application. | Verification number B (照合番号B) | しょうごうばんごうビー | A 14-digit verification value consisting of date of birth (6) + expiration date (4-digit Western year) + security code (4), used when reading front-side information via the card-face verification application. | Replay attack (リプレイ攻撃) | リプレイこうげき | An impersonation attack that resends a signature or response that was legitimate in the past. Prevented by nonce single-use and expiration. | ekyc-session-orchestration, jpki-ap-riyousha |
| Relay attack (中継攻撃) | ちゅうけいこうげき | Relay attack. An attack in which an attacker relays the victim's card reading and signature and abuses them in a different session. Countered by session binding and time constraints. | ekyc-session-orchestration |
| nonce (nonce) | ノンス | A one-time random value issued by the server. Included in challenge-response exchanges or in the signed data to prevent replay. | ekyc-session-orchestration, jpki-ap-riyousha |
| Challenge-response (チャレンジレスポンス) | チャレンジレスポンス | A holder authentication scheme in which the card signs a server-supplied challenge (nonce) with its on-card private key, and the signature is verified with the corresponding certificate. | jpki-ap-riyousha, ekyc-session-orchestration |
| Certificate chain (証明書チェーン) | しょうめいしょチェーン | The verification path from the end-entity certificate to the trust anchor. In JPKI, a self-signed single-tier CA (for details, see signature-verification). | signature-verification |

| keyref (keyref) | キーレファレンス | A reference value (P2, etc.) that specifies the target PIN or key in VERIFY and similar commands. keyrefs other than those for JPKI-AP are unconfirmed (see apdu-cheatsheet.md). | ios-corenfc-apdu, pin-lock-handling |
| certificatePolicies (certificatePolicies) | サーティフィケートポリシーズ | An X.509 certificate extension. Stores policy OIDs indicating the certificate type. The specific OID values for JPKI have not been confirmed in primary sources (see signature-verification). | signature-verification |
| Verification at the time of transaction (取引時確認) | とりひきじかくにん | Under the Act on Prevention of Transfer of Criminal Proceeds, the verification of identifying particulars, etc., required when a specified business operator conducts a specified transaction. | honnin-kakunin-houhou |
| Specified transaction (特定取引) | とくていとりひき | A transaction for which verification at the time of transaction is mandatory under the Act on Prevention of Transfer of Criminal Proceeds (opening a deposit/savings account, cash transfers exceeding 100,000 yen, etc.). | honnin-kakunin-houhou |

## Sources

This glossary is a derived reference within this plugin, and the definitions of each term are based on the following. For primary records of values and procedures, refer to the individual skills and the following.

- `references/method-map.md` (the sole authoritative source for names, designations, and effective dates of identity verification methods)
- `references/apdu-cheatsheet.md` (AIDs, APDUs, SW1SW2, PIN lengths / lockout attempt counts, and the confidence level of each value)
- `references/jpki-introduction.ja.md` (JPKI mechanisms, types of electronic certificates, validity checks, and functional enhancements; Digital Agency "Public Personal Authentication Service (JPKI)")
- <https://www.jpki.go.jp/> (J-LIS "Public Personal Authentication Service Portal Site" — general explanations of JPKI, electronic certificates, and PINs)
